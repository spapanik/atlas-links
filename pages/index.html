Atlas Links

A bookmark manager for Chrome. Save and organise web bookmarks with tags and descriptions, find them with fuzzy search, and optionally sync them through your own Google Drive. Atlas Links works locally without an account.

What Atlas Links does

Atlas Links is a local-first Chrome extension for capturing, organising, and searching bookmarks. It adds fast capture, fuzzy search over bookmark names and descriptions, tags, and a clean library. All bookmarks are stored locally on your device by default — no account required.

Capture

Save the current page with a keyboard shortcut. The title and URL are pre-filled so you can add tags and notes in seconds.

Organise

Add tags and descriptions to your bookmarks. Browse, filter, and manage your library from the side panel or a full page.

Search

Search bookmark names and descriptions with fuzzy matching, and narrow your library with tag filters.

Private sync

Optionally sign in with Google to sync your bookmarks to your private Google Drive app-data folder. No third-party servers involved.

How it works

Atlas Links runs entirely on your device. Bookmarks are saved in Chrome's local extension storage. When you choose to sign in with Google, the extension stores a single bookmark-data file in the hidden appDataFolder of your Google Drive — a private area that other apps cannot access and that does not appear in your regular Drive file list. Atlas Links never sees your Google password and has no developer-operated backend or database.

You can import existing bookmarks from your browser, export your library as a JSON file, and use every feature offline. Syncing is optional and can be turned off at any time without losing your local bookmarks.

Your data

Atlas Links does not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage data. No advertising or tracking is included. The only data it processes is the bookmark content you create and the Google Drive scope needed for optional sync. Read the full privacy policy for details.
